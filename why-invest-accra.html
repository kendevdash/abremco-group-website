6 Reasons to Invest in Accra Right Now

Ghana's capital is one of Africa's most dynamic real estate markets, and the window of opportunity is now.

Rapid Capital Appreciation

Accra's residential property market has seen consistent 10–15% annual capital appreciation in key corridors like North Legon, East Legon, and Airport Residential. Early movers capture the highest gains.

Africa's Most Stable Economy

Ghana is consistently rated among West Africa's most politically stable and business-friendly nations. Strong rule of law, transparent land title systems, and a growing middle class drive sustained demand.

Strong Rental Demand

Accra hosts thousands of expatriates, diplomats, and multinationals who command premium rents. Gross rental yields of 8–12% significantly outperform UK, US, or Canadian buy-to-let markets.

Dollar-Denominated Pricing

Arcadia View is priced in USD, protecting diaspora investors from local currency fluctuations. Your investment is held and valued in dollars, with rental income also receivable in foreign currency.

Infrastructure Boom

Major infrastructure investment is transforming Accra. New highways, the Accra-Tema motorway expansion, and business districts are driving property values in North Legon and surrounding areas higher every year.

Secure Title & Legal Protection

ABREMCO GROUP provides full title deed transfer on handover. Every unit comes with complete legal documentation, transparent contracts, and registered title, giving you absolute security over your asset.
