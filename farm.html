About ABREMCO Farm

Ghana's Premier Agricultural Investment Platform

ABREMCO Farm is the agricultural investment arm of ABREMCO Group, Ghana's leading premium real estate developer. We bring the same standard of excellence, transparency, and returns to the farming sector.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my investment guaranteed?

All ABREMCO Farm investments are contractually guaranteed and backed by physical land and farm assets. We maintain comprehensive crop insurance with certified providers to protect against natural events.

How and when do I receive my returns?

Returns are paid directly to your bank account on your chosen schedule, monthly (Harvest and Landowner tiers) or quarterly (Seedling tier) with a detailed breakdown each payment.

Can I invest from outside Ghana?

Absolutely. We welcome diaspora investors from any country. We accept international bank transfers and mobile money. All contracts and reports are available online in your investor dashboard.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes. Farm visits are included in all packages, from 1 visit per year on Seedling tier to unlimited visits on Landowner tier. Live video tours are available for international investors.

What is the minimum investment?

Investment entry amounts vary by tier and are discussed privately with your dedicated advisor during a free consultation. Our Seedling tier is designed as the ideal entry point, with access to one farm category and a 15% annual return. Contact us to receive full pricing details.
